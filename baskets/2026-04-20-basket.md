## Monday 2026-04-20 Revision — Corrected Entry Prices + Earnings-Blackout Filter

### Why the original basket needed a rebuild

Two methodology problems were caught before trading:

1. **Phantom entry prices.** The picker used `mid = (bid + ask) / 2` from Yahoo's Friday-close chain. Thinly-traded strikes carry stale after-hours asks with a bid of zero, so "mid" became fiction. The three call-side legs (GTLB $27C, DOW $44.5C, DBX $28C) all had Yahoo bid $0.00 on Friday — the reported $0.57 / $1.07 / $0.47 credits were averages of zero and a ghost ask of $1.15 / $2.15 / $0.95. A live ThinkOrSwim screenshot of the GTLB chain showed bid $0.00 / ask $0.05 — actual tradable credit would have been pennies, not $0.57. Put-side legs and HIMS had real two-sided markets and were unaffected.
2. **No earnings-blackout filter.** Nothing in the original pipeline checked whether a ticker was going to report earnings between the sale date (Mon 2026-04-20) and expiry (Fri 2026-04-24). That's a hard mandatory exclusion: earnings moves inside a 4-day short-option window are un-hedgable.

### Fixes implemented

- Picker now requires **`bid > 0`** on every candidate strike — no phantom asks survive.
- Credit estimate switched from `(bid+ask)/2` to **`bid`** — the most conservative, actually-tradable number.
- Strike-level liquidity floor of **OI ≥ 25 or volume ≥ 25** added so we don't pick a strike with a real bid but no market-maker presence.
- **Earnings-blackout filter**: if next earnings date from either TradingView's `earnings_release_next_date` OR Yahoo `quoteSummary.calendarEvents.earnings.earningsDate` falls inside `[2026-04-20, 2026-04-24]` inclusive, the ticker is **ineligible** — no exceptions, no override.
- Live Monday-open Yahoo chain pulled for the top 25 candidates per side, then strikes re-picked using Monday-live bids (the Sunday snapshot was pre-open, prices have moved).

Scripts: `scripts/_rescreen_bid_earnings.mjs` (Friday-snapshot rescreen) and `scripts/_rescreen_live_monday.mjs` (Monday-live re-pick).
